One of the biggest failures of traditional notification systems is **alert fatigue**. Every incoming message, email, or capture triggers a ding, forcing humans to act as manual triage routers.

Dori solves this with an **Automated Triage & Confidence Threshold Engine**. Instead of notifying you about every raw event, Dori categorizes incoming captures along strict confidence thresholds.

## 1. The Attention Model & Severity Levels

Dori evaluates every situation across three severity states:

- **`quiet`**: High-confidence routine actions (e.g. automatically transcribing a note, linking a file to a project, updating indexes). Runs 100% silently in the background.
- **`waiting`**: Asynchronous tasks executing on your behalf (e.g. background document parsing, research bundle generation).
- **`needs_you`**: Items that require explicit human judgment, confirmation, or decision approval.

---

## 2. Confidence Threshold Routing

When a voice memo, document, or capture enters Dori, the classification engine assigns a **Confidence Score ($0.00$ to $1.00$)** based on entity matching, project affinity, and historical filing patterns:

### High Confidence ($\ge 0.85$): Silent Auto-Filing
If Dori is highly confident about entity mapping (e.g. a meeting transcript matching an existing calendar event and project folder), it files the note directly into your Markdown vault without interrupting you.

### Medium Confidence ($0.50 - 0.84$): "Needs You" Approval Strip
If an item has ambiguity (e.g. a decision note mentioning two potential client accounts), Dori routes it to the **Needs You** strip on your home dashboard. It prepares a draft recommendation so you can approve it with one click (`⌘+Enter`).

### Low Confidence ($< 0.50$): Misfile Triage Queue
If a capture cannot be mapped to any known entity, Dori holds it in the **Triage Inbox** so you can assign it manually or dismiss it.

---

## 3. Self-Correction & Pattern Absorb

Dori monitors manual triage corrections:
* If you manually move a file from Project A to Project B, Dori's **Pattern Absorb Engine** updates its local routing heuristics.
* Future captures with matching attributes automatically hit the high-confidence auto-filing threshold, reducing manual triage effort over time.
