Accounts track organisations, not individuals — prospects, clients, proposals, follow-ups, and commercial context, plus the projects linked to each one. This sits alongside [Relationships (People & Accounts)](/getting-started/relationships): People is who, Accounts is which organisation they're with.

![Accounts Workspace Timeline](/images/features/accounts-timeline.png)

## Creating an account

Go to **Accounts → New** and fill in:

- **Account name** and a **slug** for its URL.
- **Kind** — what kind of organisation this is.
- **Domain** — its website domain, used to help match people and captures to it.
- **Relationship status** — where things stand (e.g. prospect, active client).
- **Website / reference** — a link for context.

## Where to find it

Go to **Relationships → Accounts** in the sidebar.

## Why it matters

When a person is linked to an account, captures and meeting notes involving them can surface commercial context automatically — so you're not cross-referencing a CRM separately from your notes.
