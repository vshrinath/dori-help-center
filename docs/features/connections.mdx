**Settings → Connections** is the full list of what Dori can reach — channels, meeting sources, external tools, your devices, and read-only sources like email. Voice/WhatsApp and meeting notetakers get their own pages ([Voice & WhatsApp](/features/voice-and-whatsapp), [Meeting notes](/features/meeting-notes)); connecting external MCP servers or using Dori from another tool has its own section too (see [MCP](/mcp)). This page covers the rest.

![AI Models & Connected External Tools](/images/features/connections-overview.png)

## Connect your devices

If you're running Dori Desktop, **Settings → Connections → Connect your devices** lets your phone or another device reach this instance over [Cloudflare Tunnel](/getting-started/secure-remote-access):

1. Click **Enable remote access**. Dori provisions a public `https://<slug>.mydori.app` URL for you — no Cloudflare account needed on your end.
2. Scan the QR code (or copy the URL) from another device — no app install needed on that device, just open the link in any browser.
3. Sign in with your Dori account on that device; opening the URL doesn't automatically share your session.

## Sources

A few read-only sources beyond meetings, also listed under Connections:

- **Google** — reads your Calendar events and Meet transcripts (shared with the meeting-notes setup — see [Meeting notes](/features/meeting-notes)).
- **Microsoft** — reads your Outlook calendar and Teams transcripts, same as above.
- **Gmail** — reads only the emails you label **DORI**; nothing else in your inbox is touched.
