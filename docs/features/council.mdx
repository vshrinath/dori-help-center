Council is a panel of advisor personas, each reviewing your work from a specific angle, that you can consult individually or run your work through in sequence for structured, multi-pass feedback.

![Council Advisor Personas & Pipelines](/images/features/council-panel.png)

## Advisors today

Council ships with a set of built-in advisor personas covering different review angles. Defining your own custom advisor isn't available yet — that's planned for a future release, not something you can do today.

## Consulting the council

- **Call one advisor directly** — open their page and ask them about the piece of work in question.
- **Run a pipeline** — a named sequence of advisors, where each one sees the previous stage's output, not just your original draft, useful for task types you repeat often (for example, a writing review that checks argument, then prose, then stress-tests the result).

## Where to find it

Go to the **Council** section in Dori. From there you can browse the advisors, open the **Pipelines** page to see or run a defined sequence, and check the **Craft guide** and **FAQ** for more on how Council reviews work.
