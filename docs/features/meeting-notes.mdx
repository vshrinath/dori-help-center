Meeting recordings become structured notes automatically. Decisions, tasks, and follow-ups are extracted and linked to the right people, so you're not the one re-listening to a recording to find what was agreed.

![Meeting Notes Player & Speaker Transcript](/images/features/meeting-notes-transcript.png)

## Connecting a meeting source

Go to **Settings → Connections → Meeting sources**. Each provider has its own card showing whether it's connected; you can connect more than one at once, and Dori will pick up recordings from whichever platform the meeting actually happened on.

<Tabs inline param="source">
  <Tab title="Fathom">
    Fathom is a notetaker bot that joins and records calls across any platform, not just one video tool.

    Click **Connect securely** on the Fathom card. You'll be asked to approve access — once connected, the card shows "active" and Dori starts picking up new recordings automatically.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Meet">
    Sign in with Google from the Google Meet card. Dori requests calendar and Drive read access so it can find your meeting events and the recordings/transcripts Meet saves to Drive afterward.

    If you've already signed in to Dori with Google for something else, you may just need to *upgrade* that connection to grant the extra meeting-related scopes, rather than signing in again from scratch.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Microsoft Teams / Copilot">
    Sign in with Microsoft from the Copilot card. Dori requests calendar and online-meeting read access, and reads Teams meeting transcripts through the Microsoft Graph API.

    As with Google, an existing Microsoft sign-in may only need an upgrade to grant these scopes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Once at least one source is connected, a **Sync now** button appears if you want to pull in a recent recording immediately instead of waiting for the next automatic sync.

<Callout type="note">
Zoom and Fireflies are visible in this list but marked "coming soon" — they aren't connectable yet.
</Callout>

## What you get afterward

Once a recording is available, Dori turns it into a note in your vault with:

- A summary of what was discussed.
- Decisions and action items, extracted from the transcript.
- Links to the people who attended, so it shows up on their [relationships](/getting-started/relationships) page too.

You don't need to manually forward or upload anything — once a source is connected, this happens on its own after each meeting.
