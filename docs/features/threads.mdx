A thread is Dori's record of an ongoing situation — not a single message, but everything connected to it over time: the tasks, captures, and people involved, and a running conversation attached to all of it.

## Starting a thread

From the **Add** screen, switch to intent mode ("I need to prepare for…", "I'm working on…") instead of a plain capture, and Dori starts a thread rather than filing a one-off note. Threads also form naturally as Dori links related captures and tasks together over time.

## What's on a thread

- **Members** — the tasks and captures that belong to this situation.
- **People** — anyone involved, pulled from those members.
- **Status** — active, waiting, dormant, or resolved, so you can tell what's still live at a glance.
- **A conversation** — you can keep talking to Dori inside the thread itself, with full context of everything attached to it.

## Where to find it

Go to **Library → Threads**, or open one directly from a task or capture that's linked to it.

## Why it matters

This is what turns a scattered pile of related captures into one place you can actually work from, instead of you reconstructing the situation from memory every time it comes back up. If you want to hand that context to another AI model, attach the relevant files and create a [handoff](/features/handoffs) — a separate, file-based mechanism, not something a thread exports automatically.
