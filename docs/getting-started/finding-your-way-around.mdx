## The Main Sidebar

The sidebar organizes your work into intuitive sections:

### 1. Inbox & Approvals
Your central hub for review. When Dori processes background captures or proposes external actions (like sending an email or executing an automated script), items appear here for your review and approval. See [Audit & approvals](/features/audit).

### 2. Tasks & Commitments
A unified list of action items automatically extracted from your meeting transcripts, voice notes, WhatsApp messages, and project notes. See [Automations](/features/automations).

### 3. Projects & Accounts
- **Projects**: Dedicated workspaces for active strategic initiatives, launches, and campaigns. See [Projects](/features/projects).
- **Accounts**: Isolated folders for client organizations, partners, and vendors. See [Accounts](/features/accounts).

### 4. Relationships (People & Accounts)
Your directory of colleagues, clients, advisors, and organization accounts. Dori automatically links meeting notes, visiting cards, and commitments to specific people and accounts in your vault. See [Relationships](/getting-started/relationships).

### 5. Trips
Track travel expenses and receipts for a business trip, scoped to the client or account it's billable to, through to reimbursement. See [Travel expenses without a spreadsheet](/guides/manage-trips-and-travel-context).

### 6. Library & Deliverables
Your searchable repository of generated deliverables:
- **Meeting Notes**: Transcripts and summaries of past calendar calls. See [Meeting notes](/features/meeting-notes).
- **Drafted Documents**: Proposals, SOWs, research briefs, and investor updates. See [Create & share](/features/create-and-share).
- **Presentation Decks**: Bolt Slides briefs and HyperFrames video compositions.

### 7. Settings & Connections
Configure your AI providers, pair WhatsApp, manage watched desktop folders, and set up MCP tool extensions. See [Connections](/features/connections).

## The Command Palette (⌘/Ctrl + J or /)

For fast navigation without clicking through the sidebar, press **⌘/Ctrl + J** (or type `/`) anywhere in the app to open the **Command Palette**:

- **Search Vault**: Search past meeting notes, decisions, and people instantly.
- **Ask Anything**: Query Dori in plain language (e.g. *"Summarize our last 3 calls with Meridian"*).
- **Quick Actions**: Create a new Account, start a draft, or export a HyperFrames presentation.
