## 1. Global Hotkeys & Access

You can trigger the Floating Action Bar from any application or workspace:

| Shortcut | Action | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`⌘ + Shift + Space`** | Toggle Floating Action Bar | Global (System-wide anywhere on macOS) |
| **`⌘ + J`** | Toggle Floating Action Bar | Inside Dori Desktop App |
| **`⌘ + Shift + V`** | Start Voice Dictation | Global (Instant local Whisper recording) |
| **`Esc`** | Dismiss Floating Action Bar | Returns focus to previous application |

---

## 2. Instant Drag-and-Drop Catching

You don't need to manually upload files or copy-paste text:
1. **Drag any file or web URL** (PDF, image, document, YouTube link) over your screen.
2. The Floating Action Bar presents a visual drop overlay: `📥 Drop file to capture into Dori`.
3. Drop the file—Dori automatically runs local parsing via Markitdown or Whisper and indexes the content into your vault.

---

## 3. Hands-Free Voice Dictation

Press **`⌘+Shift+V`** to record quick thoughts on the go:
* Voice audio is transcribed locally using embedded **Whisper models** on your Mac CPU at **$0 token cost**.
* Structured commitments, meeting notes, or tasks are automatically extracted and indexed into your active vault.

---

## 4. Steering & Universal Actions

The Floating Action Bar doubles as a **steering bar**:
* Type a natural language command (e.g. *"Prep for my 3 PM meeting with Alex"* or *"Draft update to investor project"*).
* Executes unified **Universal Actions** across your vault without breaking your active flow.
