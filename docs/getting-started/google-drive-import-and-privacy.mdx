## 1. Our Privacy Guarantee: Zero Automatic Scanning

When you connect Google to Dori:
* **No Bulk Reading:** Dori does **not** scan your entire Google Drive or index your personal folders in the background.
* **No Cloud Data Harvesting:** Dori does not copy your Google files to an external server or cloud database.
* **Scoped OAuth Permissions:** Dori only requests file-level access (`drive.file` / `drive.readonly` per user pick). Dori cannot see or read files you haven't explicitly chosen to import.

---

## 2. How Google Drive File Import Works

Importing a document from Google Drive is 100% user-directed:

1. **Trigger Drive Picker:** In Dori Portal or via command palette (`/`), select **Import from Google Drive**.
2. **Select Specific File:** The native Google Drive File Picker opens, allowing you to choose a specific Google Doc, Sheet, Slide, or PDF.
3. **Local Conversion:** Once picked, Dori fetches that single file, runs local conversion via embedded **Markitdown**, embeds a direct reference link back to the Google Drive original, and saves the formatted Markdown file into your local vault.

---

## 3. Disconnecting & Revoking Access

You remain in total control:
* You can disconnect your Google Account at any time in **Settings → Connections → Google**.
* Revoking access immediately halts the Drive Picker. All previously imported Markdown files remain 100% intact on your local hard drive.
