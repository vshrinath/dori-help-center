*If you haven't yet, read [The Dori Philosophy](/philosophy) to understand our core principles on model independence and token consciousness.*

Everything you need to go from understanding Dori to capturing your first note, step by step:

1. **[Life With Dori (Before & After)](/life-with-dori)** — a side-by-side story contrasting daily tool friction against effortless, context-aware work with Dori.
2. **[Installing Dori](/getting-started/installation)** — install locally on macOS or 24/7 on a Linux VPS via Docker.
3. **[Secure Remote Access with Cloudflare Tunnel](/getting-started/secure-remote-access)** — reach Dori from your phone or laptop over a public URL, no app install required.
4. **[Finding your way around](/getting-started/finding-your-way-around)** — a complete visual guide to the sidebar, inbox, knowledge layers, and command palette.
5. **[My Profile & Executive Bio](/getting-started/my-profile)** — import your LinkedIn profile and CV so Dori learns who you are and what you've built.
6. **[Relationships (People & Accounts)](/getting-started/relationships)** — understand how Dori auto-populates people, visiting cards, and client accounts.
7. **[Connect your vault](/getting-started/connect-your-vault)** — choose where your notes live and are versioned.
8. **[Choose your AI provider](/getting-started/choose-your-ai-provider)** — pick what does the thinking behind workflows, drafting, and chat.
9. **[Your first capture](/getting-started/your-first-capture)** — step-by-step walkthrough of capturing your first voice note or file.

After that, explore [Using Dori](/guides) for real-world workflows and [Features](/features) for feature specifications.
