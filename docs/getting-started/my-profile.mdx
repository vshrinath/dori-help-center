*How Dori learns who you are, what you've built, and your track record — so all drafted proposals and bios reflect your actual background.*

<Callout type="note">
**In short:** **My Profile** stores your career history, executive bio, domain expertise, and key achievements as a plain Markdown note (`work-style/my-profile.md`) in your vault.
</Callout>

While generic AI models write bland, generic context, Dori uses **My Profile** to ground its writing in your real experience, accomplishments, and professional background.

## What My Profile Powers

When Dori knows your profile, it can automatically draft:

1. **Tailored Executive Bios & "About Me" Blurbs**: Generate 50-word, 150-word, or 500-word bios for speaker intros, podcast guest forms, pitch decks, or board nominations.
2. **Updated Resumes & Executive CVs**: Re-format your accomplishments into targeted executive CVs for advisory roles, investment committees, or board seat applications.
3. **Personalized Client Proposals**: Ground proposals in your track record, highlighting relevant past projects and industry expertise.

## How to Import Your LinkedIn & CV Data

You can build your profile in minutes by importing existing professional data:

### Method 1: Export & Import Your LinkedIn Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save Your LinkedIn Profile as PDF">
    Open your LinkedIn profile page on desktop -> Click the **More** button under your headline -> Select **Save to PDF**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop PDF into Dori">
    Drag and drop the downloaded LinkedIn PDF file into Dori Desktop or a watched project folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic Profile Parsing">
    Dori parses your work history, education, skills, and accomplishments into your local `work-style/my-profile.md` file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Method 2: Drop Your Existing CV or Resume

Drop any PDF or Word document containing your resume or bio into Dori. Dori extracts your career timeline, key metrics, and project wins automatically.

## Privacy & Ownership

Your profile data lives strictly in your local vault on your Mac (`work-style/my-profile.md`). It is never added to public training data or shared with third parties. You can view or edit it anytime using any text editor.
