The **Relationships** section in Dori's sidebar organizes your human and commercial network into two connected views: **People** (individuals) and **Accounts** (organizations, clients, and partners).

![People & Accounts Directory](/images/getting-started/relationships-overview.png)

---

## 1. People

Go to **Relationships → People** in the sidebar. Each person gets a dedicated vault profile displaying their interaction history, meeting transcripts, open commitments, and linked accounts.

### Auto-Population
You don't need to manually enter contacts one by one. Dori automatically creates and updates People profiles whenever individuals are mentioned in:
- Calendar meeting invites and transcripts
- WhatsApp voice notes and text messages
- Forwarded emails and project notes

### Quick Ways to Add or Enrich Contacts
You can also add or enrich People profiles directly:
- **Drop a Visiting Card**: Snap a photo or drop a PDF/image of a physical business card into Dori. Dori parses the contact details (name, title, email, phone, company) and creates a formatted profile.
- **Share a Profile Link**: Paste a LinkedIn profile URL or company bio link into Dori. Dori fetches public background details and attaches them to the person's profile.

---

## 2. Accounts

Go to **Relationships → Accounts** in the sidebar. Accounts track client organizations, vendors, and commercial prospects.

- **Automatic Contact Grouping**: When people are linked to an account (e.g. *Priya* linked to *Meridian Corp*), Dori aggregates all notes and meetings involving anyone at Meridian under the Meridian Account timeline.
- **Commercial Context**: Store pricing terms, proposal histories, and active project statuses alongside client meeting notes. See [Accounts](/features/accounts).

---

## Why Relationships matter

Before walking into a call or writing an email, open the recipient's **Relationships** page. You'll instantly see:
- Every past decision agreed with them
- Open follow-ups and tasks assigned to them
- Commercial proposal history for their account

This prevents context switching and ensures you never forget a commitment.
