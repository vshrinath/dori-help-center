*How to reach Dori from your phone, laptop, or any other device — no app to install, just a link.*

<Callout type="note">
**Why Cloudflare Tunnel?**  
Dori runs [`cloudflared`](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/connections/connect-networks/) alongside your Engine to open a secure, outbound-only tunnel to Cloudflare's edge. That gives you a public `https://<slug>.mydori.app` URL you can open **in any browser, on any device — no app install required**.
</Callout>

---

## Why Cloudflare Tunnel Keeps Dori Private

When running Dori Desktop on your Mac or Dori Engine on a Linux VPS, your vault files, meeting transcripts, and project notes contain sensitive business data.

**With Cloudflare Tunnel:**
1. **Zero Open Ports**: `cloudflared` makes an outbound-only connection to Cloudflare's edge. Nothing inbound is exposed, and there's no router port forwarding to configure.
2. **A Real Public URL, Not a Private Network**: Unlike a mesh VPN, the URL works for anyone you share it with, on any device, without installing anything first.
3. **Your Own Login, Every Time**: Opening the tunnel URL does not sign you in automatically. Whoever visits signs in with their own Dori account, the same way they would at `app.mydori.app`.

## Setup Guide

### Step 1: Enable Remote Access

In Dori, go to **Settings → Connect your devices** and click **Enable remote access**. Dori Portal provisions a Cloudflare Tunnel and a DNS record for you automatically — you don't need a Cloudflare account or to run any commands.

<Tabs inline param="host-os">
  <Tab title="macOS (Dori Desktop)">
    Provisioning happens entirely from the Settings page. Once it finishes, your public URL appears there.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Linux VPS (Docker)">
    Install the `cloudflared` binary (the Docker installer offers to do this for you), then run:

    ```bash
    cloudflared tunnel run --token <token>
    ```

    The `<token>` comes from **Settings → Connect your devices** in Dori Portal — copy it from there rather than running `cloudflared tunnel login`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

---

### Step 2: Share the URL

Your vault is now reachable at `https://<slug>.mydori.app`. The slug is auto-assigned but renameable from the same **Settings → Connect your devices** page.

Send that link to any device — phone, tablet, another laptop. There's nothing to install on the receiving end; opening the URL in a browser is the whole setup.

---

### Step 3: Sign In on That Device

Visiting the tunnel URL is not an automatically-shared session with `app.mydori.app` or any other Dori surface. Sign in with your own Dori account on that device, the same way you would anywhere else.

<Callout type="tip">
**Pro Tip: Combined with WhatsApp for Mobile Capture**

## Troubleshooting & Security Check

- **Tunnel status**: Under **Settings → Connect your devices**, Dori shows whether the tunnel is currently active and its public URL.
- **Renaming your slug**: Change `<slug>.mydori.app` to something memorable from the same Settings page at any time.
- **Account required**: Anyone opening the URL still needs a valid Dori account — the public URL alone does not grant access to your vault.
