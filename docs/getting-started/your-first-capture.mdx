*Now that your vault is connected and your AI provider is set up, let's capture your very first note and watch Dori organize it in real time.*

<Callout type="note">
**In short:** Dori accepts input from anywhere — voice notes, dropped files, pasted links, or WhatsApp messages. You don't need to choose filenames or folder paths; Dori handles indexing and filing automatically.
</Callout>

---

## Step 1: Choose Your First Capture Method

Pick whichever capture method is easiest for you right now:

<Tabs inline param="capture-method">
  <Tab title="Option A: WhatsApp Voice Note">
    If you've paired WhatsApp (under **Settings → Connections**), open WhatsApp on your phone and record a 15-second voice memo:

    > *"Met with Alex today about the Q4 marketing campaign. We agreed to finalize the design mockups by next Wednesday."*
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Option B: Command Palette Input">
    In Dori Desktop, press **/** to open the Command Palette, select **New Capture**, and type or paste a note:

    > *"Project Launch Note: Need to schedule security audit for Zenith account before October 15th."*
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Option C: Drop a File into a Watched Folder">
    Drop any PDF, document, or audio file into your desktop's watched folder (e.g. `~/Downloads` or `~/Desktop/Dori-Drop`).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

![Quick Capture Popover Modal](/images/getting-started/your-first-capture.png)

---

## Step 2: What Dori Does Behind the Scenes

The moment your capture arrives, Dori's local background engine executes four automated steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Transcription & Parsing">
    If you sent a voice note, Dori transcribes the audio locally using speech-to-text. If you dropped a PDF or web link, Dori extracts the text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Entity & Relationship Matching">
    Dori identifies names (*Alex*, *Priya*), dates (*next Wednesday*), and topics (*Q4 marketing*), matching them against existing contacts in your vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action Item Extraction">
    If your capture contains a promise or commitment, Dori extracts it as a task and adds it to your **Tasks & Commitments** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vault Filing">
    Dori saves a clean Markdown file in your vault under `captures/YYYY-MM-DD-note.md` — permanently owned by you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 3: See Your Organized Note

Open **Inbox** or **Tasks** in Dori Desktop. Your new capture is ready:

- The voice note transcript or text is cleanly formatted.
- Mentioned contacts are linked to their **People** profile.
- Extracted tasks appear in your task dashboard.
