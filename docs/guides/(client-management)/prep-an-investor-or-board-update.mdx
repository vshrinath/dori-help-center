**For:** founders and CEOs who own a recurring investor or board update

*If you're a founder or CEO spending every month-end digging through old emails, project notes, and metrics trying to assemble a board update — this is for you.*

<Callout type="note">
**In short:** use Dori to query active project folders, recent meeting notes, and account decisions across the past month. Dori rolls up progress, key wins, and open risks into a clean, structured update draft — saving hours of manual assembly.
</Callout>

Writing monthly investor updates or board memos is a chore. The information you need is scattered everywhere: product wins sit in dev notes, customer feedback lives in call transcripts, and open risks are buried in 1-on-1 meeting summaries. Reconstructing four weeks of progress by hand routinely eats half a day — hours you spend assembling context instead of leading your company.

## How the update assembles itself

Dori continuously indexes your meeting notes, project milestones, and account decisions in your vault. When month-end arrives, Dori synthesizes updates across all active project directories into a structured update template — pulling out key wins, product releases, metrics, and open challenges.

## What's actually driving this, and what it costs

Searching project folders and pulling relevant vault notes run locally on Dori's background engine, no API cost involved. Synthesizing multi-project context and drafting the final update prose is the part that uses your connected [AI provider](/getting-started/choose-your-ai-provider).

## Questions you can ask to build your update

You can ask Dori to pull specific sections of your update directly from your vault history:

- "Summarize our top 3 product wins and customer launches from July."
- "What key challenges or risks were discussed in team meetings this month?"
- "List all new client accounts onboarded over the last 30 days."
- "Draft a 1-page monthly investor update with Wins, Metrics, Risks, and Asks."

See [Create & share](/features/create-and-share) for more on drafting from your vault.

## Quick setup (one time, about two minutes)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create & share">

## A real-world walkthrough: Assembling a July investor update

1. **Continuous Capture**: Throughout July, your team held meeting calls, logged project notes under `projects/`, and updated client accounts under `accounts/`.
2. **Generating the Draft**: On August 1st, open **Create & share** and instruct Dori: *"Draft a July update for investors covering Key Wins, Roadmap Progress, Commercial Traction, and Asks."*
3. **The Result**: Dori outputs a complete draft:
   * **Key Wins**: Highlights 2 major client deals closed in `accounts/`.
   * **Roadmap Progress**: Pulls completed features from `projects/v2-launch`.
   * **Commercial Traction**: Summarizes pilot feedback from customer call transcripts.
   * **Asks**: Identifies open hiring or intro needs mentioned in executive meeting notes.
4. **Final Polish**: Spend 5 minutes reviewing and tweaking numbers before emailing your investors.

## Why this saves hours every month

- **No Missing Milestones**: Dori references your complete vault history so small wins and product improvements aren't forgotten.
- **Accurate Context**: Pulls verbatim facts and dates directly from meeting transcripts and project folders.
- **Repeatable Routine**: Turn a multi-hour writing chore into a 5-minute review process every month.
