**For:** consultants and founders running a sales or client pipeline without a dedicated CRM

*If you're running a business or consultancy out of spreadsheets, Apple Notes, and your head because traditional CRMs feel like a separate filing chore — this is for you.*

<Callout type="note">
**In short:** create an Account for each client or prospect and link key contacts. As you hold meetings, take notes, or capture decisions, Dori links them automatically to the right account — giving you a full, searchable relationship timeline without maintaining a separate CRM.
</Callout>

Traditional CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive) force you into double entry. You take meeting notes in one app, then have to log into your CRM to update deal stages, paste summaries, and set manual reminders. Because it feels like administrative overhead — often 15-20 minutes a day of re-typing the same update in two places — team members stop updating it, and your CRM data becomes stale and unreliable.

## How your pipeline builds itself

In Dori, your client relationships, meeting notes, project specs, and contacts all live in the same Markdown vault. Relationship history isn't a separate database you have to maintain — it's a living timeline built automatically as a byproduct of your everyday work.

![Client Account Directory & Pipeline Timeline](/images/guides/accounts-list.png)

## What's actually driving this, and what it costs

Creating accounts, linking contacts, and building relationship timelines rely on local vault matching logic, running at zero API cost. Extracting key decisions, summarizing long call transcripts, and identifying action items from notes is the part that uses your configured [AI provider](/getting-started/choose-your-ai-provider).

## Quick setup (one time, about two minutes)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an Account">
    Go to **Accounts** in Dori and click **New Account**. Enter the organization name (e.g. *Acme Corp*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link Key Contacts">
    Add the primary people you deal with at that organization under **People**. Dori links them directly to the Account page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work normally">
    That's it. Any meeting recording, voice note, or document mentioning that company or contact is automatically linked to their timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A day in the life: Managing a pipeline without CRM overhead

Imagine you're managing a 3-month sales process or consulting engagement with a prospect named Meridian:

1. **Initial Call**: You host an introductory call with Priya (VP of Product at Meridian). Dori captures the meeting recording, generates a summary, extracts action items, and links the note to **Priya** and **Meridian**.
2. **Follow-up Note**: You record a 30-second voice note on your walk after the call ("Priya mentioned their main priority is launching by Q4, budget is $50k"). Dori transcribes the memo and appends it to Meridian's account timeline.
3. **Prepping for the Proposal Call**: Two weeks later, open Meridian's Account page in Dori. Every previous discussion, agreed decision, and open task is displayed in one unified view — no digging through old emails or CRM logs.

## What this looks like across 10 accounts, not one

As your account list grows, Dori keeps your context clean and structured:

- **Zero Data Lock-in**: Every account and person is stored as plain Markdown files (`accounts/meridian.md`, `people/priya.md`) in your vault. If you ever switch tools, your data remains yours.
- **No Stale Records**: Because updates happen whenever you capture a note or meeting, account histories stay fresh without manual filing sessions on Friday afternoons.
- **Cross-Account Isolation**: Information for Client A never leaks into Client B — each account maintains its own isolated timeline, linked projects, and action items.
