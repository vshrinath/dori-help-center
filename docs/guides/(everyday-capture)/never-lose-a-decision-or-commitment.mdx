**For:** founders and consultants who make real commitments on the go, between meetings

*If you've ever promised a client a follow-up while walking between meetings, or made a key product decision over WhatsApp that vanished into your message history — this is for you.*

<Callout type="note">
**In short:** send Dori a quick WhatsApp text or voice note whenever a decision is made or a promise is given. Dori transcribes, parses, tags, and files it into your Markdown vault automatically — so nothing slips and everything remains searchable months later.
</Callout>

The most important business decisions don't happen in formal docs — they happen on the go. You agree to a pricing change during a walk-and-talk, promise a client an updated proposal over WhatsApp, or outline a strategic shift in a 30-second voice memo. Because manual filing requires sitting down at a computer, these critical commitments get buried under new chat notifications and forgotten — a decision that isn't captured within the day is, in practice, a decision you'll have to make again from scratch.

## How the capture works

Dori captures decisions from whatever device or channel is fastest in the moment. Whether you send a WhatsApp message, record a voice memo on your watch, or drop a text note, Dori transcribes the content, extracts the core decision or task, tags the relevant people and projects, and files it safely into your Markdown vault.

![Tasks & Commitments Dashboard](/images/guides/tasks-dashboard.png)

## What's actually driving this, and what it costs

Audio transcription (Whisper / local speech-to-text) and text parsing are managed locally by Dori's engine, at zero API cost. High-level entity extraction and decision tagging is the part that uses your connected [AI provider](/getting-started/choose-your-ai-provider).

## Questions you can ask about past commitments

Once on-the-go captures are flowing into Dori, you can query your past decisions effortlessly:

- "What did I promise to send Priya by the end of this week?"
- "What pricing decision did we make during Tuesday's walk-and-talk?"
- "Summarize all open commitments I've made across all clients this month."
- "What did I agree to regarding the Q4 feature roadmap?"
- "Find the voice note where I discussed custom contract terms with Sam."

See [Voice & WhatsApp](/features/voice-and-whatsapp) for more capture options.

## Quick setup (one time, about two minutes)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect WhatsApp or Voice Capture">
    Go to **Settings → Connections → WhatsApp** to link WhatsApp. For local voice-memo folders, set up [folder watching](/features/folder-watching) instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test capture">
    Send a short message or voice note to Dori (e.g. *"Promised Marcus we'll send the updated proposal by Thursday"*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your Inbox">
    Dori processes the note, tags Marcus, creates an action item, and files it in your vault.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A real-world scenario: Capturing a commitment on the walk back

1. **The Walk-and-Talk**: You finish a coffee meeting with Marcus. On your walk back to the office, you record a 20-second voice note on WhatsApp: *"Decided to give Marcus a 10% volume discount if they sign by Friday. Promised to send revised contract terms today."*
2. **Background Processing**: Dori transcribes the audio, identifies the key decision (*10% volume discount for Friday sign-off*), extracts the action item (*Send revised contract terms*), and tags **Marcus** and the **Acme Account**.
3. **Six Months Later**: You're negotiating a renewal with Marcus. Instead of trying to recall what was agreed over coffee half a year ago, you search Dori: *"What discount did I give Marcus last time?"* Dori retrieves the exact decision note with date and context attached.

## Why this beats standard notes apps

- **Zero Manual Filing**: You don't have to categorize, tag, or move files into folders—Dori extracts entities and files notes automatically.
- **Unified Memory**: Voice notes, text messages, and meeting summaries live in one searchable place rather than split across separate messaging apps.
- **Action Item Resurfacing**: Promises you make don't get lost—they surface as actionable items alongside your project dashboard.
