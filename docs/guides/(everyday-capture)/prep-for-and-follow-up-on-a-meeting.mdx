*If you're the one carrying every client relationship, every commitment, and every "what did we agree on" in your own head — this is for you.*

<Callout type="note">
**In short:** connect a meeting source once (about two minutes). From then on, every meeting arrives as a note with a summary, decisions, and action items — already linked to the right person and account, and searchable months later just by asking.

Before a client call, you're digging through old email threads and Slack DMs trying to remember what was agreed last time. After the call, notes live in a doc nobody — not even future you — will ever search again. Digging through old threads to reconstruct "what did we agree last time" can eat 10-15 minutes before a call has even started. A follow-up you promised gets buried under the next thing, and it slips.

## How the meeting note builds itself

Connect a meeting source once, and every meeting from then on arrives with a summary, decisions, and action items pulled out automatically — already linked to the person and account it was with, filed under the right project without you doing anything, and searchable months later in plain language.

## What's actually driving this, and what it costs

Not everything here is an AI call. Matching attendees to people you already know is handled by matching logic against your vault, not an AI model — so it doesn't cost anything per meeting and doesn't depend on which provider you've connected. Filing a note under the right project works the same way.

The part that genuinely uses AI is turning the raw transcript into a summary and extracted decisions — that step runs on whichever [AI provider](/getting-started/choose-your-ai-provider) you've connected, and it's the one place a meeting actually costs something to process.

## Questions you can ask afterward

This is what it's actually for. Once even a handful of meetings have gone through Dori, you can ask things like:

- "What did we agree with Meridian last quarter?"
- "What's still outstanding from my last call with Priya?"
- "Summarize every meeting I've had with Meridian this year."
- "What did I promise to send after Tuesday's call with Priya?"
- "Who else was on the call where we discussed pricing with Meridian?"

You ask these the same way you'd ask a colleague who happened to be in every meeting you've ever had — see [Ask anything](/features/ask-anything).

## Quick setup (one time, about two minutes)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a meeting source">
    Go to **Settings → Connections → Meeting sources** and connect Fathom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams — whichever you actually use. See [Meeting notes](/features/meeting-notes) for the full setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="That's it">
    Nothing to configure per meeting from here on. New recordings from a connected source are picked up on their own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your first meeting with Dori running

Say you've got a renewal call with Priya at Meridian.

**Before the call.** Open Priya's page — it shows history, open threads, and follow-ups from last time, so you're not reconstructing context from memory. Meridian's linked account shows the commercial context alongside it: where the relationship stands, what's been proposed. See [relationships](/getting-started/relationships) and [Accounts](/features/accounts).

**During the call.** Nothing to do. Your connected meeting source records as usual; Dori picks up the recording afterward.

**After the call.** A structured note appears with:

- A summary of what was discussed
- Decisions and action items pulled from the transcript
- Attendees matched to the people already in your vault — Priya is matched by name against people you already have, not guessed

That note is already linked to Priya, and to the Meridian account.

<Callout type="tip">
**Pro Tip: Post-meeting voice notes on the walk back**  
Drop a 15-second voice note on WhatsApp right after walking out of a client meeting (e.g. *"Priya seemed hesitant on Q4 implementation dates, follow up with tech team"*). Dori transcribes and appends your voice note as a **Post-Meeting Impression** directly to the meeting note before you even open your laptop.
</Callout>

## What this looks like after ten meetings, not one

This is where it compounds:

- Every meeting note is scoped to its project or account automatically — you never manually file one into the right place.
- Ask what you agreed with Priya six months ago and get the answer with the source note attached, instead of scrolling through old messages.
- Follow-ups from past meetings resurface as tasks instead of relying on you to remember them.

The mental load this removes isn't the meeting itself — it's everything you used to have to do afterward to make sure it wasn't forgotten.

## Go further

- **Before a high-stakes meeting**, get a second opinion on your talking points from [Council](/features/council).
- **Want to draft the follow-up somewhere else** — ChatGPT, Claude, wherever? Bundle the meeting into a [Handoff](/features/handoffs) instead of re-explaining the context by hand.
