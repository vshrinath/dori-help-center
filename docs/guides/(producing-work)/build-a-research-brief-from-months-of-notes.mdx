**For:** researchers, analysts, and product managers synthesizing many interviews or sources

*If you're a researcher, journalist, analyst, or product manager sitting on dozens of interview transcripts, PDFs, and notes — this is for you.*

<Callout type="note">
**In short:** group your research notes, interview transcripts, and saved files into a Dori **Project**. Ask Dori to extract themes, cross-reference findings, and synthesize a structured research brief — with citations linking back to your exact source files.
</Callout>

Conducting deep research leaves you with a mess of raw material: hour-long interview transcripts, downloaded PDF reports, web clips, and quick voice memos. Synthesizing all of this into a clear research brief traditionally requires re-reading dozens of documents, copy-pasting quotes by hand, and risking missing key evidence buried in old files — re-reading a dozen interview transcripts alone can eat the better part of a day.

## How the synthesis works

Dori connects all your research material inside a single Markdown project folder (`projects/{research_topic}/`). Rather than treating files as isolated documents, Dori indexes the full text across transcripts and notes — allowing you to query recurring themes, compare viewpoints across sources, and draft a synthesis brief backed by source citations.

## What's actually driving this, and what it costs

File indexing, full-text searching, and vault source linking run locally on Dori's background engine, at zero API cost. Thematic clustering, multi-document synthesis, and prose drafting is the part that uses your connected [AI provider](/getting-started/choose-your-ai-provider).

## Questions you can ask across your research

Once your research material is inside a Dori Project, you can ask deep synthesis questions:

- "What were the top 3 recurring pain points mentioned across all 12 user interviews?"
- "Summarize everything interviewees said about onboarding friction, with quotes."
- "Compare how Expert A and Expert B view market expansion in Europe."
- "Which research notes mention security compliance risks?"
- "Draft a 2-page research synthesis summarizing key findings and recommendations."

See [Ask anything](/features/ask-anything) and [Projects](/features/projects) for more examples.

## Quick setup (one time, about two minutes)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Research Project">
    Go to **Projects → New Project** and name it after your topic (e.g. *Q3 Customer Discovery* or *AI Security Market Study*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Research Files">
    Drop interview transcripts, PDFs, or text notes into the project folder (or configure [Folder watching](/features/folder-watching) to sync them automatically).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Synthesize & Draft">
    Query your project knowledge base or open **Create & share** to generate a comprehensive research brief.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A real-world walkthrough: Synthesizing 15 customer interviews

Imagine you're conducting a 2-month customer research project:

1. **Capturing Interviews**: As interviews take place, meeting recordings and transcriptions are automatically saved under `projects/customer-discovery/`.
2. **Asking Dori to Synthesize**: At the end of the research phase, you ask Dori: *"Draft a research brief summarizing key user needs, feature requests, and workflow blockers across all 15 interview notes."*
3. **The Sourced Output**: Dori generates a structured Markdown brief featuring:
   * Executive summary of core takeaways.
   * Categorized findings with verbatim quotes.
   * **Citations**: Every insight includes a link back to the exact interview transcript file in your vault (e.g. `[Interview #4 - Marcus](file:///path/to/note)`).

## Why this changes research workflows

- **Traceable Evidence**: Every claim in Dori's research brief is tied to a specific note or transcript, making it easy to verify source material.
- **Never Forget B-Side Findings**: Insights mentioned in passing during early interviews aren't lost — Dori cross-references the entire corpus regardless of when notes were added.
- **Continuous Updating**: As you conduct 5 more interviews next month, add them to the Project folder — Dori updates its synthesis without requiring you to start over.
