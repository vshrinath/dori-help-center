*If you frequently need to send tailored speaker bios, podcast intros, "About Me" blurbs, or updated executive resumes — this is for you.*

<Callout type="note">
**In short:** Import your LinkedIn PDF export or existing resume into Dori to create a permanent **My Profile** memory file. Dori uses this profile to generate tailored executive bios and updated CVs instantly.
</Callout>

As a founder, executive, or consultant, you constantly need different versions of your bio: a 50-word intro for a podcast host, a 200-word bio for a conference brochure, or a 2-page executive resume for a board seat application. Manually editing your CV or writing new bios from scratch every time is tedious — 20-30 minutes you didn't plan for, usually right before a deadline.

## How your profile becomes a bio

Dori maintains your career history, achievements, and writing preferences in a central vault profile (`work-style/my-profile.md`). When you need a bio or resume, Dori drafts it in seconds tailored to the exact word count, tone, and audience required.

## What's actually driving this, and what it costs

Parsing your LinkedIn PDF or CV into a structured profile note runs locally on Dori's background engine, at zero API cost. Drafting a bio, resume, or questionnaire answer tailored to a specific audience and word count is the part that uses your connected [AI provider](/getting-started/choose-your-ai-provider).

## Step-by-step: Importing your LinkedIn data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download LinkedIn Profile PDF">
    Open your LinkedIn profile page -> Click **More** -> Select **Save to PDF**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop into Dori">
    Drag the downloaded PDF into Dori Desktop or paste the file path into the Command Palette (**⌘/Ctrl + J**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Profile">
    Dori creates your `work-style/my-profile.md` vault note with structured sections: Executive Summary, Work History, Education, Key Achievements, and Media Mentions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
