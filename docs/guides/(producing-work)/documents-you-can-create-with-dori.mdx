**For:** anyone drafting proposals, updates, briefs, or decks from real work history

*Starting from a blank page is the hardest part of writing — and it's the part that costs you the most, whether that's an hour lost to a proposal or a whole afternoon lost to a board update. Dori eliminates the blank page by drafting deliverables directly from your accumulated vault memory.*

Say it's a Monday like this: a client wants a revised proposal by noon, your board update is due Friday, and a teammate just asked for a one-pager on last quarter's research. Three blank pages, three different audiences, and the raw material for all of them is already sitting in your vault — scattered across meeting notes, account history, and saved research you'd otherwise have to go dig up by hand.

<Callout type="note">
**In short:** Dori doesn't generate generic AI text. Every document Dori creates is grounded in your actual meeting notes, project decisions, client histories, and saved research — formatted as plain Markdown you own.
</Callout>

Here's what actually happens behind each row.

## 1. Slide Decks & Presentations (Bolt Slides & HyperFrames)

Once a narrative, project brief, or proposal is approved in Dori, that same approved content can feed slide and video generation instead of you rebuilding it by hand:

- **Bolt Slides**: Dori generates a provider-neutral Markdown production brief from your approved narrative — preserving facts, ordering, and source references — for Bolt Slides (or another presentation tool) to turn into a deck.
- **HyperFrames**: For video presentations, Dori projects your approved narrative into a motion video composition.

<Callout type="note">
Slide and video generation are the newest part of Dori's drafting pipeline and moving fast — the exact export options are worth a quick check in-app, since they're actively expanding.
</Callout>

## 2. Investor & Board Updates

Roll up a month’s worth of project decisions, client milestones, and financial risks across all your accounts into a polished board memo.

- **What Dori does**: Scans your vault for notes tagged with major milestones or open risks over the last 30 days and drafts a structured executive update.
- **Guide**: [Prep an investor update fast](/guides/prep-an-investor-or-board-update).

---

## 3. Client Proposals & SOWs

Draft commercial proposals that incorporate exact client requirements without re-asking questions or copying figures from old emails.

- **What Dori does**: Reads the client's isolated account folder (`accounts/{client_name}/`), extracts past scope discussions, and drafts a tailored Statement of Work.
- **Guide**: [Draft anything Dori already knows](/guides/draft-anything-from-what-dori-knows).

---

## 4. Pre-Call Executive Briefs

Walk into any client meeting or sales call prepared with complete context.

- **What Dori does**: Meeting prep is proactive — for meetings on your connected calendar, Dori prepares a brief automatically ahead of time. You can also trigger it conversationally by just mentioning the meeting to Dori (e.g. "I'm meeting Ravi from Acme tomorrow at 2pm"); Dori looks up what it knows about that person and account, kicks off research if it's someone new, and gives you a link to the prep doc.
- **Guide**: [Keep every client's context separate](/guides/keep-every-clients-context-cleanly-separate).

---

## 5. Sourced Research Briefs & Market Audits

Synthesize months of scattered interview transcripts, PDF whitepapers, and web research into a cohesive document with citations.

- **What Dori does**: Reads saved research notes, groups findings by topic, extracts direct quotes, and appends original source links.
- **Guide**: [Build a research brief from months of scattered notes](/guides/build-a-research-brief-from-months-of-notes).

---

## 6. Product Specs & Technical PRDs

Turn customer feature requests, bug reports, and team discussions into structured product requirements documents.

- **What Dori does**: Aggregates customer feedback notes from watched folders and meeting transcripts into a formal PRD with user stories and technical constraints.

## How Dori drafts documents (3-step workflow)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Command Palette or Chat Dock">
    Press **⌘/Ctrl + J** in Dori Desktop or message Dori via linked WhatsApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request Your Deliverable">
    Type what you need: *"Draft an investor update for July"* or *"Export the Q4 strategy brief to HyperFrames slides."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and Export">
    Dori generates a Markdown note in your vault grounded in your real history. Edit the text as needed, or export directly to PDF, Bolt Slides, or HyperFrames MP4.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data ownership & security

All documents created by Dori are saved as **plain Markdown files on your computer** (`.md`). They are not locked inside a proprietary database. You own the files forever, and can open or edit them in Obsidian, VS Code, or any text editor.
