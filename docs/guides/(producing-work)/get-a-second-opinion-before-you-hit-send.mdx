**For:** anyone about to send a high-stakes email, proposal, or pitch

*If you've ever hesitated before clicking send on a critical client proposal, hard email, or strategy memo wishing you had an expert colleague to sanity-check it — this is for you.*

<Callout type="note">
**In short:** Dori's Council ships with a panel of built-in advisor personas, each reviewing from a specific angle. Consult one directly, or run a pipeline where several advisors review in sequence, to catch flaws and strengthen your prose before hitting send.
</Callout>

When you're drafting high-stakes material — a client proposal, an investor update, or a sensitive contract response — you need objective feedback. But asking busy colleagues for a quick review takes hours or days, while standard AI chat assistants tend to give generic, overly agreeable praise instead of sharp, constructive critique.

## How Council reviews a draft

Dori's **Council** feature gives you a panel of advisor personas, each with a distinct role and way of reviewing work. You can consult a single advisor or run your draft through a multi-pass pipeline where each stage builds on the last.

## What's actually driving this, and what it costs

Routing your draft to the right advisor and assembling background vault context are local vault operations, at zero API cost. The review synthesis and critique itself is the part that uses your connected [AI provider](/getting-started/choose-your-ai-provider).

## How to use Council for reviews

Depending on what you're sending, you can choose the depth of feedback you need:

- **Single Advisor Review**: Ask a specific advisor to evaluate your draft against one angle.
- **Multi-pass Pipeline**: Run a sequence of advisors, where each stage sees the previous one's output — for example, a logic check first, then a tone-and-clarity pass.
- **Context-Aware Feedback**: Advisors review your draft alongside background files in your vault — checking whether your proposal matches past client promises or agreed pricing.

## Quick setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Council">
    Go to **Council** in Dori.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an advisor or pipeline">
    Browse the built-in advisors and pick the one that matches what you need reviewed, or a pipeline for multi-pass review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a review">
    Paste or select your draft document and run the review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note">
Defining your own custom advisor isn't available yet — you're choosing from the built-in panel today. Custom advisors are planned for a future release.
</Callout>

## A real-world example: Reviewing a $50k client proposal

Say you're preparing a major consulting proposal for Meridian:

1. **The Initial Draft**: You write the proposal using context Dori already knows about Meridian's Q4 priorities.
2. **Consulting an advisor**: You run the draft past a skeptic-style advisor. Instead of saying "Great job!", it highlights a vulnerability: *"You promised a 4-week delivery timeline, but previous meeting notes show Meridian's IT team requires a 3-week security review phase."*
3. **Refining the Proposal**: You adjust the timeline, run a tone-focused pass, and click send with complete confidence.
