*A story following a founder and consultant through an intense, high-stakes workday — demonstrating how Dori carries context so you can focus entirely on leadership.*

<Callout type="note">
**The Story in Brief:** Share on WhatsApp, drop a voice note, or save a file — Dori organizes it, remembers it, and has it ready the moment you need it. Here is what a day looks like when your software carries the cognitive load.
</Callout>

---

## 8:45 AM — The Morning Briefing Without Tab Chaos

The morning begins under the pressure of back-to-back strategy calls. First up is Meridian, a key client account with six months of history, complex commercial terms, and open commitments scattered across email threads, legal notes, and past meeting scripts.

In the old world, this moment meant 15 minutes of frantic tab-scrambling — digging through Google Docs, searching Gmail, and trying to reconstruct past promises from memory. 

With Dori, you simply open the **Meridian Account** page. In seconds, Dori projects a unified, crystal-clear timeline: every decision reached in March, open action items, linked team profiles, and key commercial constraints. You walk into the meeting calm, completely prepared, and in total command of the context.

---

## 9:15 AM — The Meeting Where Nothing Is Lost

During the 45-minute strategic review with Meridian's leadership team, you give 100% of your presence to the conversation. You aren't distracted by frantically typing notes, missing subtle non-verbal cues, or worrying about recording every commitment.

The moment the call ends, Dori's engine goes to work in the background. Powered by on-device [Whisper](/open-source/open-source-foundations) speech-to-text, Dori transcribes the recording locally on your Mac at zero token cost. It automatically parses the raw transcript into structured sections: explicit decisions approved, commitments assigned to specific team members, target deadlines, and open strategy questions.

Without any manual filing, Dori links the meeting summary directly to Meridian's **Account page** and updates individual attendee histories under [Relationships](/getting-started/relationships). Before you even grab your next cup of coffee, Dori places a ready-to-send follow-up email draft in your inbox, drawing on your [profile](/getting-started/my-profile) and past writing. What used to take 45 minutes of tedious post-meeting admin now takes 30 seconds of quick approval.

---

## 11:30 AM — The Epiphany on the Walk Between Meetings

Stepping out of a product review onto a noisy city street, a sudden strategic insight hits you — a way to restructure a client proposal that could double its win probability. 

You don't pull out a laptop or open a complex note-taking app. You open WhatsApp, press record, and speak for 15 seconds into your phone: *"On the Meridian proposal, let's lead with the phased rollout model we used for Apex last quarter."*

Back on your Mac, Dori's background engine transcribes the voice note, identifies the mention of Meridian and Apex, extracts the strategic insight, and silently files it under the active Meridian proposal workspace. You didn't sort, tag, or file a single item — Dori adapted to your physical routine.

---

## 2:15 PM — The Ingest Stream and Token-Conscious Intelligence

A client forwards a dense 80-page quarterly financial report. You drag the PDF into your `Downloads` folder.

Instead of burning time manually uploading the document to a cloud AI chat box — and wasting tens of thousands of expensive cloud API tokens — Dori's local folder watcher detects the file instantly. Dori normalizes the PDF into clean Markdown via [Markitdown](/open-source/open-source-foundations) and indexes its contents locally on your Mac's CPU using [SQLite FTS5](/open-source/open-source-foundations) at **0 API token cost**.

When you open your chat and ask: *"What are Meridian's projected margins for Q4 based on the new report?"*, Dori performs a surgical local search, retrieves the exact tables, and presents a precise answer. You didn't pay an extra cent in API fees, and your sensitive financial data never left your machine.

---

## 4:00 PM — The High-Stakes Proposal Written in Your Voice

It's time to draft the formal Meridian expansion proposal. Normally, this requires two hours of tedious copywriting, copy-pasting company boilerplate, and editing robotic AI drafts that sound like generic corporate fluff.

Instead, you type a single prompt to Dori: *"Draft the Meridian expansion proposal using My Profile, our past Apex case study, and this morning's voice memo."*

Dori weaves together your local `my-profile.md` accomplishments, your historical project metrics, and your exact writing style rules. The resulting draft arrives in under two minutes — written in your authentic, authoritative voice, grounded in exact historical figures, and ready for final review.

---

## 6:00 PM — Effortless Context Switching and Total Peace of Mind

By the end of the day, you have switched context between six major accounts, reviewed three strategy briefs, and made dozens of commitments. 

In the past, 6:00 PM brought administrative dread: a fried brain, forgotten follow-up emails, and the nagging anxiety that an important detail slipped through the cracks.

With Dori, you close your laptop with absolute peace of mind. Dori carried the cognitive load all day. Every decision was logged, every commitment was tracked, and every note was filed into plain-text Markdown files on your hard drive — building a compounding personal knowledge asset that belongs to you forever.
