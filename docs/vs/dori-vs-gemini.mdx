## The Problem With Cloud-Only Context Windows

Google’s **Gemini** models excel at multi-modal analysis across long video files, massive codebases, and large document dumps thanks to their massive context windows. However, relying solely on cloud LLM context introduces key operational gaps:

1. **No Offline Access**: If you are on a flight or in an area with poor cell coverage, Gemini cannot search your past meeting notes, client agreements, or project files.
2. **No Background Desktop Watchers**: Gemini cannot automatically monitor your Mac's `Downloads` folder, transcribe a 15-second WhatsApp voice memo recorded on the go via [Whisper](/open-source/open-source-foundations), or extract decision commitments from local meeting recordings.
3. **Data Lock-In**: Files uploaded to Google Workspace tools are managed under Google's cloud ecosystem.

Dori provides the local, offline-first vault that captures, parses, and indexes files locally, while allowing you to use Gemini's massive context window when synthesizing large strategic projects.

## How Dori Upgrades Google Gemini

### 1. 100% Offline Vault Search
All notes, client meeting summaries, and decision logs created by Dori live as standard plain-text Markdown files (`.md`) on your local hard drive. You can search, browse, and edit your entire vault 100% offline without an internet connection.

### 2. Gemini isn't a directly connectable provider — here's the closest path
Gemini doesn't appear in Dori's own AI provider list today — see [Choose your AI provider](/getting-started/choose-your-ai-provider) for what does (Claude, Codex, Antigravity, or Grok CLIs; OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, or Groq API keys). If you specifically want Gemini's reasoning applied from inside Dori, OpenRouter's model catalog includes Google's Gemini models, so connecting Dori to OpenRouter gets you there indirectly.

### 3. Layperson File Coexistence
You don't need complex tools to use Dori with Gemini. Open your Dori vault folder in Finder and drag any `.md` file directly into Gemini Web whenever you need deep multimodal analysis. If you ever stop using Dori, **your compounding context stays on your hard drive forever**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO & Search Q&A)

### Does Google Gemini work offline?
No. Google Gemini requires an active internet connection to send queries to Google's cloud servers. Dori runs its full-text search engine ([SQLite FTS5](/open-source/open-source-foundations)) and speech transcription ([Whisper](/open-source/open-source-foundations)) locally on your Mac, allowing you to access and search your vault 100% offline.

### Can Google Gemini watch local Mac folders?
No. Gemini Web and Gemini Advanced require manual file uploads through the browser interface. Dori includes built-in folder watchers that monitor folders like `~/Downloads` or `~/Desktop`, automatically converting incoming files via [Markitdown](/open-source/open-source-foundations) into indexed vault Markdown.
