## 4 Big Advantages of Pairing Dori With Your AI

### 1. Automatic Memory Retrieval (No More Copy-Pasting)
When you use ChatGPT or Claude alone, you spend half your time acting as a human copy-paste bridge. You search through old emails, dig through Google Drive, download PDFs, and paste them into the chat window.

**How Dori changes this:**
Dori maintains a high-speed search index locally on your Mac's CPU. When you ask Dori a question — like *"Draft an executive summary for the Meridian project"* — Dori automatically performs a semantic vector search across your local vault. It instantly retrieves the exact past meeting notes, pricing proposals, and client emails, feeding them directly to your AI model behind the scenes. You never have to copy-paste background context again.

---

### 2. Instant Project & Account Context
AI models are incredible at writing and reasoning, but they don't know your business context. If you ask ChatGPT to write a contract extension without background files, it will invent generic placeholder terms.

**How Dori changes this:**
Dori organizes your scattered notes into clear **[Accounts](/features/accounts)** and **[Projects](/features/projects)**. When your AI model drafts a proposal, it grounds its response in your actual commercial constraints, team relationships, and historical project milestones. The result is a precise, tailored document that looks like it took hours of preparation.

---

### 3. Driving Real Work (Not Just Chat)
Standard AI web apps are chat boxes: they give you text on a screen, leaving the administrative burden of filing, formatting, and executing on your shoulders.

**How Dori changes this:**
Dori uses your AI model as an active thinking engine to drive real desktop workflows:
- **Meeting Follow-ups**: Local speech transcription ([whisper.cpp](/open-source/open-source-foundations)) extracts decisions and places ready-to-send email drafts in your inbox.
- **Document Creation**: Dori converts raw research into structured project briefs, financial ledgers, or presentation slide decks via [HyperFrames](/open-source/philosophical-inspirations).
- **Automated Filing**: Incoming PDFs dropped into your `Downloads` folder are automatically indexed, converted into Markdown via [Markitdown](/open-source/open-source-foundations), and linked to the correct client account.

---

### 4. Zero Token Waste & Total Model Freedom
Uploading heavy PDFs or re-sending long chat histories to cloud AI providers burns tens of thousands of API tokens on every prompt, inflating subscription and API costs.

**How Dori changes this:**
- **Token Consciousness**: Dori performs full-text indexing and search **locally on your Mac CPU for $0 free**. It only sends the exact relevant snippets to the AI model, saving up to 90% of your token usage.
- **Model Independence**: Because Dori holds your entire company memory in plain-text Markdown files (`.md`) on your computer, you aren't locked into any single AI provider. Your memory stays on your Mac forever, whichever model reads it next.

## How to Get Started

You don't need developer skills to pair Dori with your favorite AI model:
- **Using Dori Desktop**: Pick a provider under **Settings → Intelligence** — see [Choose your AI provider](/getting-started/choose-your-ai-provider) for the full list.
- **Using Outbound CLI / MCP**: Power users can connect Dori directly to **Claude Code** or **Cursor** via [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](/mcp/use-dori-elsewhere) with **$0 extra API charges**.
